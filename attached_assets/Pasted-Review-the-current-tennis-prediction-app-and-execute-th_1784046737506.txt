Validation requirements:
- Verify a new paper-trade prediction can be created.
- Verify it persists in the ledger/database.

- Verify the system can continue running unattended.

Deliverables for #121:
- Root-cause analysis of why the ledger had zero graded predictions and 111 “missed” rows.
