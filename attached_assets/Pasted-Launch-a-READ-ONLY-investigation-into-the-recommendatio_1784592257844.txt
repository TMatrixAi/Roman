==================================================
DESIGN GOAL
==================================================

Separate these into independent systems:

1. Win Probability
2. Prediction Strength
3. Upset Risk
4. Evidence Reliability
5. Recommendation Level
6. Elite Certification

These must not be derived from one another.

==================================================
1. WIN PROBABILITY
==================================================

The calibrated probability should represent only the estimated chance of winning.

It should NOT be directly modified by:

• Elite status
• Upset Risk
• Data Quality
• Recommendation labels

Suggested display bands:

50–54%
Too Close to Call

55–59%
Slight Lean

60–64%
Moderate Lean

65–69%
Clear Lean

70–79%
Strong Favorite

80%+
Heavy Favorite

These labels describe matchup strength only.

==================================================
2. UPSET RISK
==================================================

Upset Risk measures instability.

Possible inputs:

• Model disagreement
• Form volatility
• Surface mismatch
• Style mismatch
• Missing data
• Small samples
• Injury uncertainty
• Retirement risk
• Travel
• Fatigue
• Match format
• Lower-tour uncertainty

Suggested bands:

0–24
Low

25–44
Moderate

45–64
High

65+
Extreme

Upset Risk should explain uncertainty.

It should NOT rewrite the probability.

==================================================
3. EVIDENCE RELIABILITY
==================================================

Evidence Reliability measures how trustworthy the prediction is.

Possible inputs:

• Data completeness
• Data freshness
• Sample size
• Surface-specific data
• Tour relevance
• Serve/Return sample
• Ranking coverage
• Injury information
• Independent model coverage
• Calibration confidence

Suggested labels:

85–100
Excellent

70–84
Good

50–69
Limited

Below 50
Weak

Evidence Reliability should influence Recommendation eligibility—not probability.

==================================================
4. RECOMMENDATION LEVEL
==================================================

Recommendation should combine:

• Win Probability
• Upset Risk
• Evidence Reliability
• Calibration
• Model Agreement

Suggested levels:

No Strong Signal

Requirements may include:

• Probability below 60%
OR
• Extreme Risk
OR
• Weak Evidence
OR
• Severe model disagreement

Standard Pick

Requirements may include:

• 60%+
• Moderate Risk or lower
• Sufficient Evidence

Strong Pick

Requirements may include:

• 65%+
• Good/Excellent Evidence
• Low or Moderate Risk
• Strong model agreement
• Validated segment

==================================================
5. ELITE CERTIFICATION
==================================================

Elite should become a certification—not another confidence label.

Suggested requirements:

• 70%+ calibrated probability
• Excellent Evidence Reliability
• Low Risk
(or tightly controlled Moderate Risk)
• Strong independent model agreement
• Surface Elo supports
• Serve & Return supports
• Calibration passes
• Specialist supports
• No major injury uncertainty
• No forced prediction
• No fallback prediction
• No tie-break prediction
• Historically validated segment

Elite should never simply mean:

"The engine predicts this player."

Elite should mean:

"This prediction passed every quality-control gate."
