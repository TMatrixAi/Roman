Fix the prediction engine based on the ablation report.

1. Reduce or disable the Availability module in the final prediction because its rest-day, travel, injury, withdrawal, and schedule-congestion signals are lowering accuracy.
2. Recalibrate Serve/Return and Recent Form because both are about 9 percentage points overconfident. Their displayed probabilities should match their actual historical win rates.
3. Make Surface Elo, Serve/Return, and Recent Form the primary prediction signals because this core combination tested better than the full engine.
4. Reduce the influence of weak models, including Fatigue, Head-to-Head, General Ensemble, and Segment Specialist, unless testing proves they improve accuracy.
5. Continue predicting every match. Do not give strong confidence when data quality is low, but also do not default close matches to 50/50 simply because some data is missing. Low-data matches should stay at moderate confidence and be clearly labeled as lower reliability.
6. When the main models are close, always use a tie-breaking order instead of averaging everything toward 50/50:
    * Serve/Return advantage
    * Surface Elo
    * Recent Form
    * Surface-specific history
    * Ranking and competition level
    * Fatigue and Head-to-Head only as minor tie-breakers
7. Always select a predicted winner. If the available evidence supports one player, show a meaningful lean instead of defaulting to 50/50. Do not artificially inflate percentages. If a match is genuinely even after all tie-breakers, then a result such as 52/48 or 53/47 is acceptable.
8. Create an Elite Prediction tier only when:
    * Data quality is high.
    * Surface Elo, Serve/Return, and Recent Form all agree.
    * Historical accuracy for that exact tour and surface segment supports the confidence.
    * The prediction passes calibration.
9. After making these changes, run a new out-of-sample test and report:
    * Overall accuracy
    * Elite-tier accuracy
    * Accuracy by tour
    * Accuracy by surface
    * Calibration by confidence bucket
    * Accuracy by data-quality bucket
